Privacy Policy for ChipLog Pro

Last Updated: April 7, 2026

Introduction

ChipLog Pro ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our AI-powered object identification scanner application.

Data Collection

ChipLog Pro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App operates primarily on your device and does NOT:

  • Collect personal data (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Track your location
  • Link usage analytics, scans, or vault data to your personal identity
  • Share data with third parties beyond what is described below
  • Require user accounts or registration

Camera Permission

ChipLog Pro requires access to your device's camera for scanning objects. How we use camera access:

  • Capture images of objects for identification
  • Transmit images securely to our AI analysis service.

Local Storage

All scanned object data is stored locally on your device. You have full control over your data and can delete it at any time. We do not sync your data to the cloud.

AI Image Analysis

Our AI provider processes your images momentarily for the sole purpose of identifying the object. Images are NOT stored by our AI provider after analysis, and no personal information is attached to the requests.

Third-Party Services

ChipLog Pro uses the following third-party services:

  • Third-Party AI Analysis — Purpose: AI-powered component identification. Data shared: Captured images (temporarily, for analysis only).
  • RevenueCat — Purpose: Manages in-app subscriptions and unlocks premium features. Data shared: Anonymous app-user IDs.
  • PostHog — Purpose: Anonymous product telemetry to understand aggregate app usage and fix bugs. Data shared: Anonymous usage events without any link to your identity.

Your Vault contents are never sent to analytics providers.

Contact Information
